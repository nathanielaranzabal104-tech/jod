About us

An El Paso street-food classic

Súper Lonches started as a humble food truck on Copia Street and grew into one of El Paso's go-to spots for honest, flavor-packed street food. Our regulars come for the Super Colita de Pavo — a thin, breaded turkey cutlet torta stacked on telera bread — and stay for the tacos, burgers and ice-cold aguas frescas.

Everything is grilled and built to order. Friendly service, big portions, and the kind of food that keeps the neighborhood coming back week after week.

Menu & highlights

Built big, grilled to order

From our signature tortas to street tacos and aguas frescas. Prices range $10–20 per person.

Signature torta

Super Colita de Pavo

Our signature overstuffed torta on telera bread — thin breaded turkey cutlet, avocado, tomato, crisp lettuce and crema. The one locals rave about.

Street tacos

Tacos de Asada

Carne asada grilled to order on warm corn tortillas with cilantro, onion and a squeeze of lime. Served with salsa roja y verde.

Street food

Lonches & Little Macho Men

Loaded mini street sandwiches stacked high with seasoned meat. Perfect to grab a few and go.

Shrimp taco

Tacos de Camarón

Grilled shrimp on charred tortillas with creamy chipotle sauce and crisp cabbage. A fan favorite with just the right kick.

Burgers

Cheeseburgers

Hearty Mexican-style burgers with melted cheese, jalapeño and all the fixings. Big, bold and messy in the best way.

Drinks

Aguas Frescas

Cold, house-made aguas frescas — horchata, jamaica and tamarindo — plus fresh-squeezed juice to wash it all down.
